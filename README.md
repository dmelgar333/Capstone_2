
# Capstone 2: Sales Analysis - Maine, NE & Massachusetts, NE

## Project Overview

Comparing and analyzing sales data for EmporiUm's two Northeast territories to provide marketing strategy recommendations

**Territories:** Maine, NE | Massachusetts, NE

---

## What This Project Does

Compares sales performance across two regions by answering:
1. Who manages each territory and what stores are included?
2. What is the monthly revenue trend for in-store sales?
3. Which stores are top performers?
4. Who are the top customers?
5. What product categories perform best?
6. Where should we focus marketing next quarter?
